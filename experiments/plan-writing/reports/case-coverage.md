## Rules and limits

- Briefs never share a run of 8 words with the document body (body excludes the metadata header and title line). A context item that quotes the body for 30% or more of its segments is cut where the quote starts. Process documents and templates are attachments, not brief text.
- Issue and pull request bodies are their current text, which authors may have edited after opening; task-checkbox and release-target lines, which record progress, are removed from briefs.
- Continuation cases: up to 3 per document, spread over eligible headings; prefix at least 100 words, reference 30–4000 words. Boilerplate sections (copyright, IPR, references, addresses, contents) and sections repeated verbatim earlier in the document are never references.
- Heading detection in PDF text (NASA, CERN, HS2) relies on numbered headings and is heuristic; numbered list items can be mistaken for sections.
- PM cases carry `needs_review`: the session map locates candidate provenance, not assent. Reviewed windows are in `pm_case_windows.json`. The Codex history behind plan-delivery is not mounted in this environment.
- Context fetch errors are listed per source in `data/context/<source>.status.json`.
