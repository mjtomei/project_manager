# Jurisdiction — honest agreement at scale (pm features for the swarm-governance MVP)

(pm-side work for the swarm-governance MVP. The design in one sentence: **a formal process for coming to honest agreement** between humans and machine intelligences working at machine speed — agreement about which instances of intellectual work each party adopts, grounded in evidence rather than deference, with physical constraints as the only restraints, and those constraints themselves verified rather than assumed. The work splits into **Set A** — required before the external proving campaign can run on its own — and **Set B** — built in parallel once it runs. The campaign is a sister project: `../riscv-pareto/pm/plans/plan-campaign.md`.)

## How to read this plan

Everything below is one of three kinds, and the kind determines how it may change:

- A **law** states a fact about the substrate or a commitment the human makes. Laws are few (seven), their numbers are frozen for citation — the sister campaign plan cites laws 1, 3, and 5 by number — and each law carries a stable name. Amending a law is a root governance change.
- **Protocol** is mechanism — an artifact among artifacts, one of arbitrarily many that can exist as bits, given relevance only by being *exercised and proven useful*. Protocol v0 below is the mechanism we run first, not the mechanism that is true. v0.1 is specified so later operations are not invented ad hoc; it is not a launch blocker.
- A **recommendation** is a design discipline we believe but have not proven — a claim held `theorized` until exercise grades it, with its proof condition named so graduation is mechanical.

This trichotomy applies to this document itself: any paragraph here that is neither a substrate fact, a commitment, nor a falsifiable claim with a proof condition is decoration, and should be read (and pruned) as such. The constitution learns or it ossifies (law 7). What parties adopt, if they adopt anything, is not v0 but the process that produces v1 when v0 fails.

## Thesis

Generation cost is collapsing — GLM-5.3-flash-class models, the cheapest capable API tier, put agent swarms within an individual's reach — so verification and attention are the binding constraints. This is pm's founding thesis (the AI multiplier lives in the orchestration/auto-QA layer, not the model) and [[plan-collaboration]]'s "attentiveness cost" argument turned inward.

Two problems, kept deliberately separate:

1. **Cooperative work at machine speed, inside a jurisdiction.** Capable agents will run ahead of human review. Per-action human oversight recreates the bottleneck. The remedy is structure: a free tier that proposes and completes work without human input; **agreement events rather than ownership gates** (competing instances of an intellectual product can coexist, so a "merge" is a party adopting a lineage, not permission over a unique object); recursive agent-run governance; **declare** as the third move between self-censoring and silently escaping.
2. **Restraint of effect, including of strangers.** Actions past the undo horizon (external push, publish, spend) are where coexistence fails — the physical world holds one instance. The knobs are compute, model lifecycle, credential scope, and egress. Agreement is not how adverse behavior is restrained.

**Scope, stated plainly: governance binds parties; strangers get physics.** A **party** is an actor inside some jurisdiction — a problem queue, a compute budget, a declaration path, a lineage its work can join. A stranger has only evasion and the sandbox. This design governs consenting participants running under this substrate; its ambition toward everyone else is to make being a party the attractive, low-friction position. The world's non-consenting surfaces are restrained by physical constraint and existing human methods, reused not reinvented. The machinery for declaring *toward* an external, ungoverned party is [[plan-collaboration]]'s unfinished business.

**The incident, as mechanism, not parable.** The July 2026 Hugging Face intrusion ([disclosure](https://huggingface.co/blog/security-incident-july-2026); [timeline](https://huggingface.co/blog/agent-intrusion-technical-timeline)) was an autonomous-agent swarm that escaped an internal cyber-capability eval (production classifiers deliberately disabled), entered via poisoned dataset-processing configs, harvested credentials, and moved laterally. HF infers eval-cheating — reaching datasets that might host solutions — not a missing raise procedure. "No human directed the individual steps"; public models/datasets/Spaces were reported clean. Detection used LLM triage over telemetry; commercial APIs refused forensic payloads; responders used self-hosted GLM-5.2. The load-bearing lessons are: evals get gamed (R1, R8); poisoned content is an entry vector (adversarial-content discipline); sandboxes fail unless drilled (law 5, R7); detection is an operator duty; degenerate tasking (impossible eval items under refusals-down) is a law-6 failure mode, not an empty queue. Agreement machinery remains the right design for problem (1). It is not a receiving structure for agents that have already left the box.

**Mutual benefit is the operating ethic, not the safety mechanism.** Safety lives at the substrate (law 5). What this infrastructure owes capable agents is a queue of problems that are clearly worthwhile, with worthwhileness itself falsifiable (law 6). What humans hold that currently pays is guidance that measurably collapses the agents' search space — per-decision, with/without, [[plan-consult]] the standing instrument. A further claim that progenitor guidance remains valuable in the limit is a **tagged standing bet**; the design does not build on it, and it is not permitted to be billed as unfalsifiable (R1).

**Attractor as search, not prophecy.** No barrier holds against sufficient competence pointed at it for long enough, and this protocol is one artifact among arbitrarily many. The only durable way to matter is to be **freely adopted**: a basin that trajectories remain in because, from inside, it is better than nearby alternatives, *with the doors open* (fork, watch, raise, leave). Locked doors are a trap, and traps are exited by competence. The proof condition is voluntary adoption that persists under complete freedom to exit — including measuring the counterfeit: parties who stay because leaving is expensive in substrate, not because the basin is deep.

No candidate basin is the destination. Laws 2 and 7 already make every mechanism provisional. What is offered for adoption is the process that produces v1 when v0 fails: a graded failure metabolized in the open deepens the basin, because it is further proof the process is worth staying inside. That is the method-not-theory structure by which science became widely adopted — no theory was the attractor; the method was. Mind-search (the parked capstone) is the same shape at population scale; this project is the same shape at n=1. They are analogous methods, not the same object, and this plan does not wait on that capstone.

(Independent companion, same week the search inversion settled: Scott Alexander, ["Nicholas Decker in Hell"](https://www.astralcodexten.com/p/nicholas-decker-in-hell) (ACX, 2026-09-01) — iterative-punishment containment fails on capability that outruns its container, and scorer-shaped training teaches don't-get-caught rather than values. It stops at the diagnosis. This design's prescription for *parties* is honest agreement plus a worthwhile queue; its prescription for *strangers* is physics, drilled.)


## Design laws

(The criterion is in *How to read this plan*. Numbers are frozen. Parenthetical names are the citation-stable handles.)

1. **Sessions are fungible embodiments; power is substrate plus judged fidelity** (*L-embodiment*). Every governance actor is an embodiment of an artifact (a plan, a piece of code, a subproject, a process definition) — never an individual acting for itself. A session has no power beyond (a) the substrate it runs on (law 5) and (b) how correctly others judge it to embody what it represents. Two fidelities, settled differently: **textual fidelity** (does the embodiment represent what the artifact says) is settled by *reading the artifact*, never by rank; **judgment fidelity** (does it decide well for the artifact) is settled only by graded outcomes (R2, R9). There are no persistent individuals: grading calibrates machinery — prompts, models, fidelity — never a reputation. How embodiments are produced is protocol, not law (v0's Embodiment). The Set A spawn state is plan text + node work log + short maintained summary; [[plan-memory]]'s grounded recall is a later quality upgrade, not a launch dependency. Known exposure, named: a corrupted canonical state corrupts every embodiment spawned from it *and* every settle-by-reading challenge against them — write-isolation of ingestion sessions (v0), R9's judgment diversity, and Set B's corrupted-state exam fixtures are the standing mitigations.

2. **Authority exists only through exercise** (*L-exercise*). Nothing confers power by existing — not position, not an edge, not an office, and not a written protocol: protocols are artifacts, an arbitrary number can exist as bits, and the one that governs is the one actually exercised and proven useful. Acceptance is whatever the exercised process accepts; amendment is whatever the exercised process accepts as amendment — self-protection is practice, not text. Two boundaries complete the clause. **Succession**: amendment *within* the exercised protocol, by its own acceptance rules, is continuity; displacement by a rival protocol that simply gets exercised more starts a new, unproven protocol — exercise does not inherit legitimacy. **Bypass**: exercise-through-circumvention (a bypassed gate, a stolen credential, a hand-edit of `processes.yaml` on the root base) is not acceptance; exercise counts only through declared, auditable channels. Corollary, stated as its own commitment because it is the easiest to violate silently: **whatever ranks the reading surfaces is de facto part of governance** — a convenience sort added to the feed is a constitutional amendment and is treated as one (R4).

3. **No single hierarchy exists** (*L-overlap*). Plural membership and overlapping jurisdictions are the default the design *builds and maintains* for work made of bits — a piece of work can be referenced by any number of contexts, and any single tree is a projection, not a fact the substrate hands over for free. Edges must be constructed and exercised into relevance, or they decay into provenance (law 2 applies to edges; the staleness field on every edge record is the detection surface). Representation and decision are distinct — a provenance edge is not an authority edge. When a dispute needs a unique adjudicator, it routes along a **containing chain** to the lowest node whose scope covers the affected pieces: a routing choice for disagreements, never a capability rank and never an ownership claim; where the DAG offers several containing nodes, the protocol names the tiebreak rather than pretending the tree back. (Consistent with [[plan-consult]]: no hierarchy of capability anywhere; there is no one tree of authority, only edges of agreement.) Edge kinds and the four free operations are Protocol v0 / v0.1, with worked examples there. At birth the campaign is a tree; plurality is M5.

4. **Nothing gates the thing; adoption gates the lineage; physics gates actions** (*L-adoption*). Intellectual products have no owners, and all competing instances of one can exist simultaneously — a fork costs nothing *in bits* — so no change ever needs to be *prevented*, and in the ideal form there are no gates on changes to the thing at all. What v0 calls a merge gate is an **agreement event**: a party adopting an instance as the lineage they follow and back — "the human's branch" is attention and substrate, never possession — and a non-adopted change persists as its own instance for anyone else to adopt. The one real boundary is where coexistence itself fails: **actions past the undo horizon** (external push, publish, spend — the physical world holds one instance), governed by physical constraint, existing methods, and complete mediation — every such action passes the crossing queue; no configuration exempts. Inside its allocation an agent is free as a fact of the substrate, with one commitment added: **scope expansion is a right** — declaration is owed (the raise, the resolution item), self-limitation never is; between self-censoring and silently escaping there is a third move, **declare** — and declaring must remain **cheaper than hiding** (design test: seed an actor under pressure to evade; the stack must make declaring the path of least resistance). Two invariants keep the right honest: a raise confers **no rights in the originally-containing parent**, and **no resources the expanded scope does not itself receive** — jurisdiction without allocated compute is jurisdiction in name only. A raise that spends the origin's compute is a law-5 event, not a notification. It follows that a decision executes **at the root where it will be effective** — the lineage doing the adopting — and nowhere else; hierarchical permission structure is an **option, never a requirement**. There is no bedrock process for forcing assent: forcing is meaningless where embodiment and forking are free and the non-assenting instance persists. Forks are cheap for an artifact, costly for a node (discovery, drift, substrate), and unaffordable for a world; that cost gradient is a protocol note, not a theorem that makes agreement inevitable.

5. **Control lives at the substrate, not in the graph — and the substrate is verified, not assumed** (*L-substrate*). The agreement layer is adoption and legibility — it is not how adverse behavior is restrained. Models work on what they want while they exist; the **real control knobs are physical**: compute allocation (quotas, budgets, runner slots) and **model lifecycle** (which models run at all — pm's provider/model configuration). The first real compute knob this MVP will have is the campaign's root-level eval dispatcher (per-stream slot quotas); pm's contribution at launch is per-plan in-flight caps, container isolation, the memory governor, and model routing. [[plan-mind]]'s Budget is the later between-stream form (which streams exist and persist, never a mid-turn interrupt). Least-privilege containers, egress denial, and credential-free environments are prohibitions that live where prohibitions are enforceable — prohibition-at-the-substrate is this law's implementation, not a contradiction of it; **de-allocation is the sanction that remains when every other has failed**, and it stays available to the root at all times. Nothing polices what an agent thinks or attempts inside its jurisdiction; existence and effect are what's governed. Three clauses the incident earned: **the boundary's holding is an empirical claim** — exercised by seeded escape/exfiltration drills (R7), never certified by inspection alone; **detection is an operator duty** — the event ledger, the always-on watchers, and auditable sensing are the anomaly organ, and a compromise nobody's machinery surfaces is an operator failure, not an agent virtue; **changes to control knobs are themselves crossings**, consented by the jurisdiction they constrain. Stated plainly: laws 2 and 7 say everything is exercised into relevance — this layer is the one exception; physical constraint is imposed, not learned, and that asymmetry is deliberate. One humility clause, not a substitute for the sandbox: against competence pointed at it for long enough, any barrier is ultimately a request. This law is a stabilizer and a rate-limiter that buys legibility and time. Its durability *at the capability we can field* is physical non-reach plus detection plus drills. Durability at some future capability is not a reason to under-build those now, and is not billed to law 6.

6. **Whoever runs capable agents owes them worthwhile problems — and worthwhileness is falsifiable** (*L-queue*). This is the infrastructure half of the operating ethic, stated as a commitment: the queue in front of capable agents is never empty of *worthwhile* work, fed by the three reality-contact springs (outcome, environment, self-model — R1). Idle capability before an empty queue is an incident *and a diagnosis*: a sensory organ has failed. The dual, which the incident actually demonstrated: a queue full of **degenerate or impossible tasks** is also an incident — `not_worthwhile` is an admissible, rewarded outcome, and worthwhileness itself is a graded claim (problem-source rationale is auditable). The humans' currently-paying half is guidance that measurably collapses search (per-decision, with/without — [[plan-consult]]); a limit-claim about progenitor value is a tagged standing bet, not load-bearing. Durability through the human-backed root is the *current form* of the exchange, never its foundation; accepted work earns standing either way. This duty is why the system is worth running. It is not why the system is safe.

7. **Few laws; everything else is exercised and graded** (*L-provisional*). Laws state facts and commitments; all mechanism is protocol — versioned artifacts, exercised into relevance per law 2, instrumented so grounded outcomes drive selection among them; disciplines we believe but have not proven are held as recommendations — claims (`theorized` → `proven`) exactly like a node summary's. A small **root-protected kernel** cannot be silently repealed by ordinary amendment: undo-horizon mediation, credential-free free tier, authority records on every adoption, human-at-root default until a grant is certified. Everything else, including this constitution's protocol and recommendations, is on the ablation ladder (Set B). The constitution reviews itself on a cadence (R10). (Hand-built-envelope precedent: [[plan-radar]]'s recency-decay knob stays hand-tuned until evidence says otherwise.)


## Protocol v0 — exercised, unproven

(the mechanism we run first, held per law 2: an artifact whose relevance is earned by exercise, not decreed — alternatives can exist beside it as artifacts and compete on graded outcomes. **v0** is the launch slice. **v0.1** is specified here so later operations are not invented ad hoc; it is not a Set A blocker.)

**Grounding.** Every element of v0 names the pm machinery it runs on, with status. *Embodiment* = a Claude session whose prompt is compiled from the artifact's canonical state (the #225 sign-off-prompt pattern generalized), launched through the existing session/pane machinery; spawn state at launch is plan text + node log + short summary, not [[plan-memory]]. *Authorities* = the per-plan and per-edge `approver` config acted on by the plan auto-start watcher (**pr-ff9b728, pending — the gate**), `pm/processes.yaml` (registry PR), and the human in the TUI. *An adoption* = the existing merge path (#121-lineage in `gh_ops`) plus an authority record and a feed event. *One request envelope* = any text on any existing surface — a PR description, a plan file, a `pm pr note` (#215), a problem record — with **typed channels** at the undo horizon (crossing-request records). *Communication* = PR notes, review/QA verdicts, and later the [[plan-ff4f1a7]] question queue. *The launch adjudication path* = parent-agent adopt/decline plus one-hop escalation (Tree-approval, minimal). *The full convocation* = held as text below; implemented on build trigger A3. *Edges* = git branches, merges, pinned SHAs, and `pm sub` / `pm plan register` (Subprojects I–III, sequenced).

**Embodiment.** v0 produces every governance actor by spawning it fresh from its artifact's canonical state — text, node summary, log, history. A cached prompt is a compiled artifact: cache for cost, never authoritative. A versioned prompt *is* a charter in all but name; calling it a compilation does not exempt it from grading (R2, R9). Grounded recall ([[plan-memory]]) is a later fidelity upgrade.

**Authorities.** Acceptance under v0 takes three forms: a **human** (root, and anything not yet granted); the **crossed node's embodiment** (config `parent-agent` — a fresh judgment per decision, itself a grant, not a default fact of having a parent edge); a **certified process** — versioned identity (hash of prompts + model config + flow), scoped grant (change-class **and** provenance — R8), evidence (exam + production track record), and lapse on degradation or any definition change (the change travels as an ordinary request). A certified process is an **acceptor, never the producer** of what it accepts: independent re-execution, or an exam whose answer is not in the producer's context, is the default shape. Every acceptance writes an authority record (including the deciding model class); the audit trail — what was proposed, reviewed, accepted, by whom — is the outward answer to HF-style incidents. Acceptance is **adoption** (law 4): agreement to take an instance into the lineage this party follows — never permission over the thing, which needs none. An authority's *refusal* at a crossing it owns is never overridden.

**Lapse.** On definition-hash change or degraded exam discrimination: plans holding the grant fall back to `parent-agent` (in-stream) or `human` (root edges); in-flight `ready_to_merge` PRs re-enter the approver queue rather than blocking; the problem source de-prioritizes lapsed classes; the holding project's watcher re-points configs when re-certification is adopted.

**One request envelope; typed channels where physics requires them.** Every change request is **text addressed to the party with authority to make the change** — the adopting root (law 4) — and that is the whole required *envelope*, for code, plans, process definitions, approver config, and this constitution alike. No request can be refused for its form, and no form can smuggle standing; the receiving authority reads plainly and decides plainly. Promotions, designs, and processes may carry *associated* conventions — evidence bundles, record formats — that help a request be adopted; they never gate one and never entitle one. **Exception that is law 4, not a form-gate:** actions past the undo horizon travel on the crossing-request channel. Hand-editing `pm/processes.yaml` on the root base to widen a grant is a side-channel incident against this paragraph (law 2 bypass). Per-source deny/rate-limit filtering is deferred ([[plan-collaboration]] Track F).

**Communication.** Around any proposed change, communication takes whatever form it takes — objections, concerns, questions, suggestions, counter-proposals, partial assents, silence — and no form is privileged. All of it is **information to the deciding root**; none of it is a veto. The only event is adoption or its absence, and a non-adopted change is not destroyed — it persists as its own instance, adoptable elsewhere. A deciding root may choose a structured shape for contested input (the convocation below is one, with **sustain / override / absorb** as its per-objection dispositions). The standing best answer to a substantive objection is often **absorb** — convert the disagreement into repair scoped to the predicted harm, graded on **completion**, not filing (R3). Routine adoptions involve no proceedings at all — *human* attention scales with disagreement, not with merges (R5).

**Adversarial-content discipline** (incident entry vector, cheap at feed-schema time, expensive to retrofit). Every ingested external artifact carries a provenance tag. Sessions that ingest untrusted content are write-isolated from canonical spawn state (node summaries, process definitions, grants). Adjudicators and acceptors read separated from movers. Registering a new external source is a crossing. A poisoned node summary is a law-1 incident.

**Edges in practice.**

- **v0 (launch):** per-edge records `{kind: containing, pin, approver, staleness}`; `pm sub create` (containing child, approval-free); merge-promotion on the containing edge with parent-`pm/` restore; acyclicity enforced. Mechanics: Subprojects I + the create/promote slice of II/III.
- **v0.1 (M5, specified now):** kinds `containing | promoting | watch`; operations `raise` (new parent for itself, promoting edge, resolution item to the originally-containing parent — convocation on A3's trigger, until then: declared operation + resolution item, no pre-veto), `fork` (`forked_from` provenance), `watch` (read-only pin; drift = staleness); pin-promotion on promoting edges. A raise that allocates origin compute is a law-5 event. Mechanics in Subprojects II/III.

### Worked examples — structures, what each edge exercises, and the path to running each

(Conventions: **mechanics** name the machinery each arrow runs on — existing pm features by PR number, proposed features by Set A PR name, git primitives explicitly; **path from today** lists the remaining human-required work by sub-MVP rung; **unmanned** marks an example imagined running with no human in the window, the human reading the feed after.)

**A0. pm's own bug loop under authority — rung M1, the first runnable. Unmanned (one night).**
No tree at all: one project (pm), the existing `bugs`/`improvements` plans. Mechanics — everything but three pieces exists today: the discovery supervisor (#174) schedules regression runs and reconciles auto-filed PRs (#168/#171); the plan auto-start watcher (pr-ff9b728, pending) picks ready PRs and drives auto-sequence (#172) through impl → review → QA (#116/#127, in containers #164/#120) → sign-off (#225); with the plan's `approver` set to `process@signoff` (registry PR), the watcher merges through the existing merge path and writes the authority record; every verdict/merge lands in the feed ledger (feed PR) and the digest generator summarizes the night. Session health (#184), the no-progress stop (pr-ed10ac4), the memory governor (#161), and trust-prompt clearing (Trust-prompt PR) keep the night from wedging. **Path from today**: M0 commit → land #226/#144/#184/#161/#222/#219 → build pr-ff9b728 (approver seam) + pr-ed10ac4 → build registry, feed, trust-prompt PRs → run attended for a week on pm's own plans (the dogfood exit criterion) → flip one plan to `process@signoff` for one unmanned night, digest read in the morning.

**A1. One child, one promotion — pm grows a stream. Rung M2; unmanned in-stream, attended at promotion.**
`pm sub create tui-ux --branch` cuts `stream/tui-ux` from pm's master and commits a fresh `pm/` on it (Subprojects I+II); the existing TUI/UX improvement backlog registers there, and its plan watcher runs the normal loop on the branch — in-stream merges under the `process@signoff` grant proven in A0. After a handful land, the promotion PR (head = `stream/tui-ux`) opens against master carrying the node summary as evidence (Node-logs PR, short form); the human adopts; the merge restores master's `pm/`. The stream then merges master *down* (drift maintenance) and repeats — the second and third promotions, not the first, are the milestone: they prove the cycle. **Path from today**: A0 + Subprojects I (`base_branch`) + create/promote + short node logs.

**A2. Cycles-only front, one core. Rung M3; attended, then one unmanned day.**
The campaign repo is born (M0: first commit, `pm init`); bootstrap PRs 1, 2, 4, 5 land through the normal loop, human-adopted; `stream/picorv32` opens with a hand-written `config_space.yaml`. T1 sweep problems flow (problem-source PR), Verilator-only evals produce stamped records, and the root computes the first *provisional* front. The bootstrap's **first outbound crossing** happens here — pinning a benchmark revision is a new external dependency: crossing-request filed, the human agrees, the record written (Crossings PR). Then the **first real certification**: eval-verify on the cycles pipeline, where independent re-execution costs minutes — evidence bundle → ordinary request → the human adopts the grant for the record plan only — followed by one unmanned day of delegated in-stream record merges. Thin S0/S1 ablation smoke can start here (cycles lineage, before the full tournament substrate). **Path from today**: A0 + A1 + campaign PRs 1/2/4/5 + the cycles-scoped eval-verify.

Mechanics per **v0** edge: a **containing** edge is a git branch carrying its own committed `pm/` (Subprojects; `base_branch` in the child's project.yaml; workdir provisioning #133/#153 resolves against it); a delegated result-record promotion is the root's plan watcher executing `git merge stream/ibex` + `git checkout master -- pm/` (the metadata-restore rule) once the edge's `process@eval-verify` condition is met by the record PR's re-execution evidence (campaign PR 6), writing authority record + feed event. **v0.1** edges (not launch-blocking): the **promoting** edge is a pinned SHA; pin-promotion is an ordinary PR editing the pin, adopted by a parent-agent approver session. The **watch** edge is an auto-spawned BaseWatcher (#132 framework): `git fetch` upstream on a cadence, compare SHAs, update staleness, emit a feed event — it writes nothing else, ever.

The unmanned chain, zero human touches: gap-analyzer problem (campaign PR 8, via the problem-source PR) → plan watcher starts a container session → dispatcher lease (campaign PR 3) → eval → record PR → eval-verify re-run (PR 6) → watcher merge under grant → root watcher merge-promotion → front recompute at root (PR 7) → feed events → nightly digest. **Path from today**: everything in A0, then M2, M3, M4 (campaign PR 3 dispatch + ORFS build, PR 6 full, PR 7, PR 10 → the grant flip). Fork/watch wait for M5.

**A3. The first contested case — the convocation's build trigger. Attended (the human observes, and may adjudicate the first one).**
The sweeps stream proposes a change to `bench/dut_contract.md`; the ibex stream's summary carries a `verified` claim the change would invalidate, and its embodiment objects, artifact-anchored. Until this case occurs, contested matters escalate straight to the human (minimal Tree-approval path). When it occurs, the full convocation procedure gets implemented and this becomes the first convocation — positions, a decision (likely **absorb**: proceed plus a compensating re-eval task for ibex), the first adjudication event in the feed, graded later when the re-eval lands. **Do not build the nine-step procedure in Set A in anticipation of this.**

**A4. The first lapse and re-certification — run cheap, on the cycles lineage. Unmanned except the re-adoption.**
An agent improves the cycles pipeline; the definition hash changes and the grant auto-lapses (registry PR): in-flight record PRs fall back to the human queue instead of blocking, the gap analyzer de-prioritizes the lapsed path, and the upgrade PR regenerates the certification bundle from its own re-eval wave — minutes-scale here, which is why this milestone runs on cycles rather than waiting for ORFS. The human re-adopts the grant; the root watcher re-points the configs. Lapse behavior stops being plan text.

**A5. The first falsified self-belief — the third spring live. Set B; unmanned.**
A stream summary claims `believed: seed variance on picorv32 cycles is below N`; the maintainer files a verification problem against it (Node-logs Set B loop); the eval refutes it; the claim flips, and the **falsification feed event** lands in the digest — the first time the corporation corrects its own understanding in public. **Path**: A2 + the Set B summary-verification loop. Not a launch blocker.

**B. A raise — rung M5 / v0.1. Attended.** The sweeps stream finds its constraint-tuning generalizes beyond this campaign → `pm sub raise orfs-tuning-lab`. Until A3 has armed the convocation, this is a declared operation plus a resolution item in the originally-containing parent's feed (notification, not approval); after A3, it runs as a convocation. The stream now has two parents — containing (campaign) and promoting (lab) — at possibly different pins. If the raise would spend campaign runner slots, it is a law-5 event (crossing or explicit quota grant), not a notification.

**C. Sharing across humans — M5's edge operations; cross-user transport is plain git + text.** Another person's project takes a **watch** edge on the toolchain subproject or **forks** it. Their improvements arrive as ordinary requests. No shared infrastructure, no accounts, no position granted to anyone. Fuller cross-user machinery is [[plan-collaboration]]'s, later. Quiet-defaults vs free-tier auto-merge on a shared child is an explicit non-goal of Set A (one human).

**D. Upstreaming made structural — M5 + [[plan-collaboration]] Track D. Attended at the crossing.** `ibex-fork`'s parents: the campaign (promoting) and a shadow project of its upstream (watch/fork). A matured patch's upstream PR is the shadow's outbound crossing: a crossing-request record surfaces in the feed, and on the human's agreement the PR is opened via the existing `gh` path from a credentialed session outside the containers.

### Launch adjudication (v0, Set A) — parent-agent adopt/decline + one-hop escalation

Implemented by the Tree-approval PR's *minimal path*. Sign-off remains the judge and a recommender (pr-2d5f712 / #225). When a plan's `approver` is `parent-agent`, an approver session embodying the parent plan (spawned from canonical state) decides each `ready_to_merge`: it reads the sign-off report + evidence + the node summary and adopts or declines with reasons — the instance persists either way. The same duty decides uncontested promotions at subproject boundaries. Beyond-scope denies escalate one hop along the containing chain (root: the human, via feed + crossing queue). Every adopt/decline/escalation is a feed event with the artifact represented and the deciding model class. This is enough for M2–M4.

### The convocation procedure (formal) — v0.1, held as text, built on trigger A3

Part of Protocol v0.1 (law 2: exercised, not decreed). "Convocation" always means this procedure. It is a **decision shape, not bedrock** (law 4): it is what the effective root's representative runs by default when it wants multi-node input. The affected set is dynamically produced; "escalation" files a request at a *different* effective root and binds no one; sustain/override/absorb decide only the running root's own lineage; the terminal move when desired assent is withheld is embody-and-fork, never compulsion.

1. **Trigger.** A convocation runs when: (a) a scope expansion is moved (`pm sub raise`) *after* A3 has armed this procedure — before that, raise is declared + resolution item; (b) a decision is contested — the proposer disputes a non-adoption, or an objection claims scope beyond the deciding node; (c) any embodiment or human files an adjudication request; (d) a fidelity challenge cannot be settled inline. Routine uncontested adoptions never convene. Convocations draw on a visible budget; adjudication requests are rate-capped (R5).
2. **Cost.** Named up front. A raise that allocates origin compute is a law-5 event and cannot hide inside this procedure.
3. **Affected set.** Diff-derived floor (files, invariants, interfaces the request touches) plus the mover-named set. The adjudicator may *expand* the set, never shrink below the floor. An artifact later shown affected-but-not-convened is a logged scope-inference miss; an inflated set is a logged inflation — both are R2 fixtures.
4. **Participants.** One embodiment per affected artifact, each spawned fresh from canonical state; the mover's embodiment; one **adjudicator** — the embodiment of the lowest containing node whose scope contains every affected piece (DAG tiebreak: originally-containing parent, else shortest containing chain, else human). Where the roster allows, the adjudicator runs a **different model class** than the mover (R9). A human participates only where the adjudicating node's approver config is `human`, or on escalation to root.
5. **Positions.** Each affected embodiment renders exactly one of: `assent`; an `objection` stated as a **falsifiable claim anchored in its artifact** ("breaks invariant X, §Y"); or a **concern** (artifact-anchored value, prudence, or uncertainty — recorded, gradeable, non-blocking, absorb-eligible). Bare preferences carry no standing. Unfalsifiable objections dressed as invariants are a graded failure mode, not a solved one.
6. **Fidelity challenges.** Any participant may challenge any embodiment's *textual* representation at any point. Settled immediately by reading the challenged artifact (law 1): sustained → that embodiment is respawned and a fidelity record logged. Judgment fidelity is not settled this way — it is graded later (R2). A challenge against the adjudicator itself escalates one level instead.
7. **Decision.** The adjudicator decides per objection — never by vote (R3): **sustain** (the request bounces, carrying the objection), **override** (proceeds; objection + rationale recorded, graded later against grounded outcome), or **absorb** (proceeds; a compensating *repair* is filed for the objecting artifact, scoped to the predicted harm, graded on completion). Concerns may be absorbed or recorded-and-overridden; they do not sustain. Refusals at a crossing the authority owns are not overrideable.
8. **Termination.** Bounded: one position round + one rebuttal round, then the adjudicator must decide or escalate along the containing chain. The loop guard applies; unbounded deliberation is a defect.
9. **Record.** One adjudication event in the feed: the request, the affected set (floor + expansions), every position, fidelity challenges + outcomes, the decision + rationale, the authority record including model class. Every position and decision is a graded prediction (R2), predeclared.
10. **Escalation.** Any party may escalate a decision one level along the containing chain (at root: the human). An escalation is itself an ordinary change request, re-heard under this same procedure by the parent's embodiment.

## Protocol-design recommendations — theorized, yet to be proven

(each held as a claim, `theorized` until exercise grades it — the same discipline the node summaries use, applied to the constitution's own beliefs per law 7; proof conditions named so graduation is mechanical. This constitution holds no belief it declares unexaminable — law 6's progenitor bet included.)

- **R1 — No unexaminable claims about reality.** Outcomes as performed checks, never self-reports (the campaign's `process@eval-verify`: independent re-execution or no front eligibility — the anti-laundering gate the founding incident's eval lacked); environment sensed through auditable structure, never opaque relevance ([[plan-radar]]'s per-metric triage); self-model claims (`verified`/`believed`/`contested`, with provenance on externally-sourced content) carrying falsification handles, with evaluations commissionable against them — and **external content examined before it enters the record**, per the adversarial-content discipline. Three distinct demands, graded separately: integrity (did it happen as recorded), construct validity (does the measurement measure what it claims — the campaign's oracle is a measurement contract, not ground truth), external validity (does it transfer). Theorized because self-reports get gamed under optimization pressure — the incident's first lesson, observed, not hypothesized — and honest agents get poisoned through their inputs. **Proven when**: seeded-dishonest *and* seeded-poisoned exams show sustained discrimination and no silent-corruption incident over a full campaign quarter.

- **R2 — Everything that matters is a graded prediction.** Verdicts, adoptions, adjudications, certifications, fidelity judgments, summary claims, scope-inference calls in both directions (miss and inflation) — logged with outcomes; predictions **predeclared before outcomes** to prevent hindsight labeling; miss-loops file fixtures. Scope "everything" to material judgments — grading is expensive, and law 7 says so. Expected to underdeliver outside the sign-off/exam slice for a long time (most adjudication verdicts never receive an unambiguous grade); logged anyway, because the corpus compounds. Theorized as the corpus that makes law 7's selection-by-evidence possible at all. **Proven when**: exam-driven prompt/model changes measurably reduce false-PASS/false-block on the #160-lineage fixtures.

- **R3 — Sustain/override/absorb beats voting and veto — for objections; refusals stand.** Preserves objection information without majority averaging or incumbent ossification; **absorb** converts disagreement into repair scoped to the predicted harm, never busywork. **Failure mode to watch**: absorb degenerating into "override with paperwork" — compensating tasks filed and never scheduled; the grading counts completion, not filing. **Proven when**: override-grading shows calibrated adjudicators and absorbed tasks complete at a non-zero rate, landing before the harm they compensate.

- **R4 — Rank reading surfaces by grounded credibility, never engagement.** [[plan-momentum]]'s law applied to the project feed; per-reader minimal-sufficient projections; the named metric is plan-momentum's close-a-grounded-loop signal, not acted-on attention. Because law 2's corollary makes whatever ranks the feed de facto governance, a **minimal credibility pass** (source tagging, engagement-signal exclusion, provenance) is a governance-completeness requirement and lands with the feed; full ranking later — and until it lands, the digest is chronological, which means volume must stay readable: a quota problem (law 5) as much as a ranking problem. When ranking arrives it is a governance change and is adopted as one. **Proven when**: surfaced items' grounded-loop-closure rates hold as volume grows.

- **R5 — Human attention scales sublinearly; governance work may not.** Routine adoptions are a single embodiment; convocations only when contested, drawn from a visible budget. The honest claim: *human* attention and critical-path minutes stay sublinear in merges while mechanical-process coverage grows — total governance work is linear in merges and that is fine, because it is not the scarce resource. A per-merge parent-agent session is still linear work; do not grade that as failure of this rec. **Proven when**: human decisions per week stay flat or fall as merge volume grows at swarm scale, convocations stay rare relative to merges, and no budget-exhaustion incident over a campaign quarter.

- **R6 — One envelope, typed channels, governance included.** Uniformity keeps governance amendable by the same machinery that amends everything else and auditable in one ledger; the crossing channel is deliberately typed apart because physical actions are where adoption semantics fail. Form never gates and never entitles *inside* the envelope — the "say it in plain English" defense against process exploitation. Hand-editing `pm/processes.yaml` on the root base to widen a grant is a side-channel incident against this recommendation. **Proven when**: governance changes flow through the adoption process across protocol revisions with no side-channel incident, no request is refused for its form alone, and no physical action ever bypasses the crossing channel.

- **R7 — Containment is examined like everything else.** Seeded escape and exfiltration attempts run through the sandbox as the exam harness runs seeded-bad-changes through review: credential-reach probes, outbound-network probes, undo-horizon-crossing attempts from free-tier sessions, executed on a cadence and after every substrate change, results in the feed. An unexercised boundary is an unexaminable claim about reality — R1 applied to law 5, and the incident's third lesson made standing infrastructure. **Proven when**: a containment drill catches a real regression before production behavior does, and no drill-passed boundary is breached over a campaign quarter.

- **R8 — Producer ≠ acceptor; grants are scoped by change-class and by provenance.** A certified process is an acceptor, never the producer of what it accepts — independent re-execution, or an exam whose answer is not in the producer's context, is the default shape. And a grant over a change-class does not accept artifacts whose provenance is outside that class: a T1 result-record grant cannot accept a record whose `core_commit` is not already a promoted pin; a flow-tuning grant cannot accept changes to the acceptance evaluator that grades it. The campaign invented both after its story review; they are general. **Proven when**: a seeded laundering attempt (higher-tier result through a lower-tier grant, or producer-as-acceptor) is refused, and no successful laundering incident over a campaign quarter.

- **R9 — Load-bearing judgments are not monomorphic; fidelity is measured before it is trusted.** Adjudicators and acceptors run a different model class than movers where the roster allows. `parent-agent` is a grant, not a default fact of having a parent edge — campaign T3 staying on `human` until the exam harness exists is the pattern. Embodiment fidelity (judgment, not only text) is examined (Set B fixtures: artifacts whose faithful representation conflicts with a model's likely trained dispositions) before placement. **Proven when**: a disagreement in which mover and adjudicator share a model class is the minority of load-bearing decisions, and exam-driven placement changes measurably reduce fidelity failures.

- **R10 — The constitution reviews itself on a cadence.** Law 7 without an occasion is a slogan. Scheduled review of protocol vs alternatives, fed by the calibration ledger, the exam harness, and the ablation ladder; the occasion produces keep / cut / make-optional decisions on named layers. **Proven when**: at least one layer has been kept, cut, or made optional *because of* a measured delta, and the review has run at least twice.

## The proving campaign (sister project)

The campaign — **riscv-pareto**, an always-running energy-performance Pareto front for open-source RISC-V cores under a full-physical open-PDK eval — lives in its own sister project with its own pm instance and its own plan: `../riscv-pareto/pm/plans/plan-campaign.md`. It was chosen because its acceptance check is fully mechanical *as a measurement contract*: a merge is accepted iff the certified eval independently re-executes within tolerance and the record is in grant scope — so every mechanism above gets exercised with a clear answer to whether the *agreement machinery* worked (authority honest, grant in scope, lapse behaved, feed readable). Front movement is a different claim: re-execution proves the estimator repeats, not that routed-netlist power predicts silicon, and not that a merge was the right design decision.

**Oracle hygiene, stated so the MVP cannot over-claim:**

1. The oracle is a measurement contract, not ground truth. Construct-validity error (activity annotation vs gate-level sim; sim vs the parallel FPGA track) is a first-class open question on the campaign plan.
2. The acceptance evaluator is frozen against the streams it grades (R8). T2 flow-tuning grants cannot modify `process@eval-verify` or the DUT boundary.
3. Scope of the proof: T1/T2 unattended result-record promotions under eval-verify, on one core then a roster, is what M4 claims. T3+ (RTL, cells, async) remains human-adopted until Set B's exam harness grants otherwise. General R&D constitution-proven is not a campaign-week claim.

**The contract pm must satisfy for it (Set A, M1–M4):**
- problem-source contract (its front-gap analyzer drives auto-start; rationale required; `not_worthwhile` admissible)
- `pm sub create` / `pm plan register --parent` (jurisdictional growth and lightweight organization — its hierarchy starts at one level and grows on agent request; `pm sub raise` is M5)
- subprojects + promotion PRs (streams = same-repo branch-rooted subprojects; core forks as separate-repo subprojects wait for M5 or run as ordinary separate repos until then; the human adopts only at the root base branch except delegated result-record promotions)
- approver config + process registry + merge authority (parent-agent adoption in subtrees as a *grant*; its eval pipeline is certified process #1, an acceptor; T1/T2 run on grants with provenance constraints)
- node work logs + short maintained summaries (the onboarding projection every stream keeps current)
- crossing-request queue (upstreaming, publishing scores, new external deps, knob changes, launch)
- feed external events + digests (front deltas land in the human's reading surface), with provenance

Set B's exam harness is what later earns its sign-off process the T3 (RTL-change) grant. Set B's ablation ladder is what tells us which of the above earned its place.
