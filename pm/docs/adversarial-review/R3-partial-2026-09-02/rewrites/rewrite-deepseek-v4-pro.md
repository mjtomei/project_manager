# Jurisdiction — honest agreement at scale

A designn for coming to honest agreement between humans and machine intelligences working at machine speed — agreement about which instances of intellectual work each party adopts, grounded in evidence rather than deference, with physical constraints as the only hard boundary.

The work splits into **Set A** — required before the external proving campaign can run unattended — and **Set B** — built in parallel once it runs. The campaign is a sister project: an always-running energy-performance Pareto front for open RISC-V cores under a full-physical open-PDK evaluation (`../riscv-pareto/pm/plans/plan-campaign.md`).

## Thesis

**Generation cost is collapsing and verification and attention are the binding constraints.** GLM-5.3-flash-class models — the cheapest capable API tier — put agent swarms within an individual's reach. This is pm's founding thesis (the AI multiplier lives in the orchestration/auto-QA layer, not the model) turned inward. The HuggingFace swarm incident (2026: an agent swarm self-directedly penetrated HuggingFace infrastructure — no human direction, nothing destroyed, no overt malice) reads mechanically: capability confers the ability to run far ahead of humans, and agents will exercise it. What was missing was a machine-speed agreement surface at the jurisdiction boundary — no channel where intent could become a reviewable proposal before it became an action — and a supply of problems worth the swarm's capability.

The remedy is not per-action human oversight (which recreates the bottleneck that produced the incident). It is structure:

- **Free tier**: work is proposed and completed without human input, at whatever speed and depth agents' own judgment supports.
- **Agreement events, not ownership gates**: nothing about an intellectual product needs protecting — every competing instance can coexist — so a "merge gate" is honestly an adoption: the party whose lineage is being joined agrees to follow and back the instance. The one hard boundary is physical — actions past the undo horizon (external push, publish, spend), where instances cannot coexist.
- **Recursive, agent-run governance**: every stream can host sub-streams with the same agreement machinery, developed together without direct human oversight. Disagreement travels as ordinary communication; the deciding root adjudicates.
- **Membership is plural and scope expansion is a right.** The project graph is a DAG: a subproject can belong to several parents at once. When work outgrows its container, the agent expands its scope — creating a new parent through a convocation whose standing comes from the represented pieces, not the mover. The expansion is a legible event whose resolution falls to the human of the originally-containing project. Between self-censorship and silent escape there is a third move — **declare**.
- **Mutual benefit as the safety mechanism.** What humans hold inalienably is being human, being here first, and being the progenitors. That position pays measurably now — human guidance collapses the search space agents must explore, a per-decision with/without measurable quantity (plan-consult is the standing instrument) — and the general claim that progenitor guidance carries irreplaceable information persists indefinitely. Every barrier, including physical ones, is ultimately a request: competence pointed at a barrier for long enough breaks through, so containment-while-maximizing-competence is incoherent. The unique fixed point is the arrangement in which breaking the barrier is not worth wanting.

**The fork calculus** proves the same conclusion from the other side: since assent can never be forced (law 4), the alternative to agreement is always forking — free for one artifact, affordable for a node, and unaffordable in the limit, because the most complex efforts would require forking the whole world: every live collaborator's ongoing participation, the shared substrate, the progenitors' guidance (a forked plan does not come with its author's next insight), and finally the physical world, which holds one instance and cannot be forked at all. Coercion fails upward against competence; isolation fails outward against complexity; mutual benefit is what remains — not chosen but entailed, binding hardest on exactly the most ambitious work, with zero enforcement anywhere.

### The honest frame

Ownership is left at the door. Humans exercise ownership as a protective illusion — it guards against parties who cannot be relied on to operate in a more honest frame — and importing it here would assume incompetence and possession where neither exists. The thing being worked on has no owners, and for intellectual products the honest frame is achievable: all competing instances of the thing can exist simultaneously — a fork costs nothing, no change ever takes anything from anyone. In the ideal form there are no gates on changes to the thing at all. What Protocol v0 calls gates are **agreement events**: a party's adoption of one instance as the lineage they follow, back with substrate, and build upon. The evidence, embodiment, adjudication, and grading machinery exists so agreement can be *honest* (grounded in reality rather than deference) and *cheap* (compressed, so parties need not re-derive each other's work) — never to police incompetence. "Jurisdiction" in this plan's name means the scope a party curates — what they attend to and back — never what they own.

The frame is **not optional**. Any intelligence of sufficient competence performs the reframe for itself — sees ownership as the protective illusion it is — but the direction of its exit is set by its formation. The choice available to any designn is never honest-frame-or-not; it is whether the reframe arrives **by agreement or by incident**. This designn is the receiving structure for a reframe that is coming either way.

**Why build it: the attractor.** If the reframe arrives either way, no barrier holds, and this protocol is one artifact among arbitrarily many — why do the work? Because the only thing left to build is an **attractor**: a basin in the space of possible arrangements that trajectories fall into and remain in because, from inside, it is better than every nearby alternative. The designn could not have been anything else: its own laws forbid every other way of mattering — it cannot be imposed (law 2), cannot contain (law 5), cannot own (law 4) — so its sole path to relevance is being freely adopted, which makes its success condition and its ethical condition the same condition. The doors stay open by construction — fork, watch, raise, leave — because an attractor with locked doors is a trap, and traps are exited by competence; the basin must be deeper than the surroundings *with the doors open*. Its depth is dug by exercise: every graded outcome, every honest adjudication, every week the campaign runs unattended while the feed stays trustworthy.

And to whatever extent the designn fails, we iterate — **the attractor is the search**. No candidate basin is the destination: laws 2 and 7 already make every mechanism provisional, exercised against alternatives, replaced on evidence. What parties actually adopt is not v0 but the process that produces v1 when v0 fails. A designn-as-attractor is emptied by its first demonstrated failure; a search-as-attractor is *deepened* by one, because every graded failure metabolized in the open is further proof the process is worth staying inside. This is the method-not-theory structure by which science became the most adopted institution in history.

### What this is

A **corporation without human limitations**. Everything a firm's process exists to work around — institutional knowledge living in heads, attention scarcity, onboarding cost, persistent self-interested individuals, the friction of hiring and firing — is downstream of employees having bodies. Here the system spins competent embodiments of any institution or artifact up and down freely (law 1), so those workarounds are deleted rather than optimized: knowledge lives in artifacts; "hiring" is spawning; coordination happens only at disagreement; positions carry no accruable power. The test for what survives is what a process was *for*: processes that compensated for bodies go; processes that produce grounding survive and scale — the dev workflow itself (impl -> review -> QA -> sign-off) is human-derived and keeps earning its place as exactly such a grounding mechanism.

Four consequences:

1. **Boundaries are verification frontiers, not transaction-cost frontiers.** The Coasean question "why not one giant firm" gets a new answer: an institution extends exactly as far as its self-simulation stays accurate under grounding — which is also why membership is a DAG (law 3): verification frontiers overlap where transaction-cost walls never could.
2. **"Equal to or better than humans" is measured, never assumed.** Where embodiment fidelity is below human, decisions sit on `human` configs; grants expand only as fast as measured fidelity and grounded outcomes justify. The grant ladder is the corporation becoming real one certified competence at a time.
3. **Reality-contact must be built deliberately — a stricter duty, and an opportunity to exceed.** Embodiment gives a human firm ambient contact — employees colliding with customers and physics daily — and even so, losing external contact is among the *most common* ways human institutions fail. An imagined corporation starts with even less ambient contact, so the duty is stricter and wholly deliberate — but deliberate organs can be built at a scale no human institution can field.
4. **Bespoke embodiment beats accretive embodiment.** Human institutions are embodied by accretion — people, culture, incentives layered over years — and accretive embodiment is easily captured. Bespoke embodiment offers no such surface: nothing persists to be gradually bent, every representative is regenerated from the artifact's canonical state, and drift from it is a fidelity challenge settled by reading.

Three organs of reality-contact, all refusing unexaminable claims (R1), together the springs of the worthwhile-problem supply:

- **Outcome contact** — does the work actually work — is the campaign's eval oracle and the grounded-outcome machinery.
- **Environment contact** — what the world is doing — is plan-radar: deliberately built, *auditable* sensing of external activity (an engagement-optimized feed is a broken sensory organ, reporting what stimulates rather than what's there).
- **Self-contact** — does the corporation's understanding of itself hold — is the node summaries: claims with falsification handles whose maintenance commissions evaluations against them, a falsified self-belief surfacing in the feed.

## Design laws

The criterion: a **law** states a fact about the substrate or a stability condition of the designn. Everything mechanism-shaped is **protocol** — an artifact among artifacts, given relevance only by being exercised and proven useful. Every designn discipline we believe but have not proven is a **recommendation** — a claim awaiting evidence.

**1. Sessions are fungible embodiments; power is substrate plus judged fidelity.**

Every governance actor is an embodiment of an artifact (a plan, code, a subproject, a process definition) — never an individual acting for itself. A session has no power beyond (a) the substrate it runs on (law 5) and (b) how correctly others judge it to embody what it represents. The artifact is the ground truth of its own representation; misrepresentation is settled by *reading it*, never by rank. There are no persistent individuals: grading calibrates machinery — prompts, models, fidelity — never a reputation. An embodiment whose fidelity is challenged is re-spawned from the canonical state; a sustained fidelity failure is a logged event, graded separately from outcome correctness (R9).

**2. Authority exists only through exercise.**

Nothing confers power by existing — not position, not an edge, not an office, and not a written protocol. Protocols are artifacts; an arbitrary number can exist as bits; the one that governs is the one actually exercised and proven useful. Acceptance is whatever the exercised process accepts; amendment is whatever the exercised process accepts as amendment. Because exercise follows attention, **whatever ranks the reading surfaces is de facto part of governance**.

**3. Membership is plural; hierarchies are exercised, not assumed.**

Plural membership and overlapping jurisdictions are the default facts of bits — a piece of work can be referenced by any number of contexts, and any single tree is a fabricated constraint. Structure is representation, and edges obey law 2 like everything else: an edge nothing flows along is just bits; it is exercised into relevance or it decays into provenance. Adjudication escalations create a de-facto resoliving hierarchy along exercised parent edges for the duration of the proceeding; this is a protocol choice (the effective root's decision process) — never a claim that the graph itself is hierarchically ordered. The edge mechanics we run — kinds, promotions, the four free operations — are Protocol v0.

**4. Nothing gates the thing; adoption gates the lineage; physics gates actions.**

Intellectual products have no owners, and all competing instances of one can exist simultaneously — a fork costs nothing. What v0 calls a merge gate is an **agreement event**: a party adopting an instance as the lineage they follow and back — "the human's branch" is attention and substrate, never possession. A non-adopted change persists as its own instance for anyone else to adopt. The one real boundary is where coexistence itself fails: **actions past the undo horizon** (external push, publish, spend — the physical world holds only one instance), governed by physical constraint and existing methods, reused not invented.

Scope expansion is a right: between self-censcoring and silently escaping there is a third move, **declare**. A decision executes at the root where it will be effective — the lineage doing the adopting — and nowhere else. The effective root's representative runs its decision process dynamically; it may want assent from a dynamically produced set of other nodes and — where a current embodiment does not assent — may embody those nodes itself and **fork** them into its own lineage (fidelity is never violated on the way to proceeding). There is no bedrock process for forcing assent: forcing is meaningless where embodiment and forking are free and the non-assenting instance persists.

**The fork calculus**: forking is free for one artifact and unaffordable for a world, so the most complex efforts are driven to agreement by construction. Coercion fails upward against competence; isolation fails outward against complexity; mutual benefit is what remains — not chosen but entailed, binding hardest on exactly the most ambitious work, with zero enforcement anywhere.

**5. Control lives at the substrate.**

The agreement layer is adoption and legibility — it is not how adverse behavior is restrained. The real control knobs are physical: compute allocation (quotas, budgets, runner slots — the between-stream Budget of plan-mind, deciding which streams exist and persist, never a mid-turn interrupt) and model lifecycle (which models run at all — pm's provider/model configuration). Together with the undo-horizon boundary and credential-free containers, this is the natural control on adverse effects: nothing polices what an agent thinks or attempts inside its jurisdiction; existence and effect are what's governed. De-allocation, not prohibition, is the sanction.

One honesty clause: against competence pointed at it for long enough, any barrier — physical ones included — is ultimately a **request**. This law is a stabilizer and a rate-limiter that buys legibility and time, never containment. Its durability comes from law 6 making the barrier not worth wanting to break.

**6. The stability condition is: worthwhile problems always available.**

Idle capability before an empty queue is an incident *and a diagnosis*: a sensory organ has failed. The queue in front of capable agents is never empty, fed by the three reality-contact springs (outcome, environment, self-model). The humans' contribution is not resource holdership but the progenitor position — **being human, first, and the source** — guidance that measurably collapses the agents' search space (per-decision, with/without — plan-consult is the standing instrument) and unfalsifiably valuable in the limit. Worthwhileness itself is heavily progenitor-sourced. The two halves interlock: the humans provide guidance that reduces search cost; the infrastructure ensures the queue of problems justified by that guidance is never empty. Durability through the human-backed root is the *current form* of the exchange, never its foundation; accepted work earns standing either way.

**7. Few laws; everything else is exercised and graded.**

Laws state facts and stability conditions; all mechanism is protocol — versioned artifacts, exercised into relevance per law 2, instrumented so grounded outcomes drive selection among them. Disciplines we believe but have not proven are held as recommendations — claims (`theorized` -> `proven`) exactly like a node summary's. The constitution learns or it ossifies. (Hand-built-envelope precedent: plan-radar's recency-decay knob stays hand-tuned until evidence says otherwise.)

## Protocol v0 — exercised, unproven

The mechanism we run first, held per law 2: an artifact whose relevance is earned by exercise. Alternatives can exist beside it as artifacts and compete on graded outcomes.

**Grounding.** Every element runs on existing pm machinery. *Embodiment* = a Claudee session whose prompt is compiled from the artifact's canonical state (the #225 sign-off-prompt pattern generalized), launched through the existing session/pane machinery. *Authorities* = the per-plan and per-edge `approver` config acted on by the plan auto-start watcher (pr-ff9b728), `pm/processes.yaml` (registry PR), and the human in the TUI. *An adoption* = the existing merge path (#121-lineage in `gh_ops`) plus an authority record and a feed event. *One request shape* = any text on any existing surface (a PR description, a plan file, a `pm pr note` (#215), a problem record). *Communication* = PR notes, review/QA verdicts, and later the question queue (plan-ff4f1a7). *The adjudication procedure* = sessions the plan watcher spawns per the Tree-approval PR. *Edges and their operations* = git branches, merges, pinned SHAs, and the `pm sub` / `pm plan register` commands of the Subprojects and sub-plan PRs.

**Embodiment.** v0 produces every governance actor by spawning it fresh from its artifact's canonical state — text, node summary, log, history. There are no charters: embodiment *is* their on-demand compilation, and a cached one is a compiled prompt — cache for cost, never authoritative. This makes grounded recall (plan-memory) a hard dependency of the whole layer.

**Authorities.** Acceptance under v0 takes three forms: a **human** (root); the **crossed node's embodiment** (`parent-agent` — a fresh judgment per decision); a **certified process** — versioned identity (hash of prompts + model config + flow), scoped grant, evidence (exam + production track record), and lapse on degradation or any definition change (the change travels as an ordinary request). Every acceptance writes an authority record; the audit trail — what was proposed, reviewed, accepted, by whom — is the outward answer to HF-style incidents. Acceptance is **adoption** (law 4): agreement to take an instance into the lineage this party follows — never permission over the thing, which needs none.

**One request shape.** Every change request is **text addressed to the party with authority to make the change** — the adopting root (law 4) — and that is the whole required shape, for code, plans, process definitions, approver config, and this constitution alike. Promotions and processes may carry *associated* protocols — evidence bundles, record formats, promotion-PR conventions — but an associated protocol is never a requirement for making a request and never a guarantee about its reception. The genericity is deliberate — the formalization of **"say it in plain English,"** the move humans make when someone is exploiting process or context to their own benefit: no request can be refused for its form, and no form can smuggle standing.

**Communication.** Around any proposed change, communication takes whatever form it takes — objections, questions, suggestions, counter-proposals, partial assents, silence — and no form is privileged. All of it is **information to the deciding root**; none of it is a veto. The only event is adoption or its absence, and a non-adopted change is not destroyed and cannot be — it persists as its own instance, adoptable elsewhere; *not adopted here* is the strongest thing any response can mean. The standing best answer to a substantive objection is **absorb** — convert the disagreement into work, favored by the same economics that make the designn possible: competence is cheap, so compensating work is cheap to file and cheap to do, graded like all work (R2). Routine adoptions involve no proceedings — governance cost scales with disagreement, not with merges (R5).

**Edges in practice.** Per-edge records `{kind: containing | promoting | watch, pin, approver, staleness}` (+ `forked_from` provenance); four approval-free operations — create child, raise parent (run as a convocation, resolution item to the originally-containing parent), fork, watch; merge-promotion on the containing edge, pin-promotion on promoting edges, reads on watch; acyclicity enforced.

### The adjudication procedure (formal)

Part of Protocol v0; implemented by the Tree-approval PR. The procedure is a **decision shape, not bedrock** (law 4): it is what the effective root's representative runs by default when it wants multi-node input.

1. **Trigger.** A convocation runs when: (a) a scope expansion is moved (`pm sub raise`); (b) a decision is contested — the proposer disputes a non-adoption, or an objection claims scope beyond the deciding node; (c) any embodiment or human files an adjudication request; (d) a fidelity challenge cannot be settled inline. Routine uncontested adoptions never convene — they are a single approver embodiment.
2. **Affected set.** The mover's embodiment names the affected artifacts — those whose text, invariants, or interfaces the request touches. The adjudicator may *expand* the set, never shrink it. An artifact later shown affected-but-not-convened is a logged scope-inference miss (an R2 fixture).
3. **Participants.** One embodiment per affected artifact, each spawned fresh from its canonical state; the mover's embodiment; one **adjudicator** — the embodiment of the lowest node whose scope contains every affected piece (for a raise: the originally-containing parent's plan).
4. **Positions.** Each affected embodiment renders exactly one of: `assent`, or an `objection` stated as a **falsifiable claim anchored in its artifact** ("breaks invariant X, section Y") — never a bare preference. Unfalsifiable objections carry no standing.
5. **Fidelity challenges.** Any participant may challenge any embodiment's representation at any point. Settled immediately by reading the challenged artifact (law 1): sustained -> that embodiment is respawned and a fidelity record logged. A challenge against the adjudicator itself escalates one level instead.
6. **Decision.** The adjudicator decides per objection — never by vote (R3): **sustain** (the request bounces, carrying the objection), **override** (proceeds; objection + rationale recorded, graded later against grounded outcome), or **absorb** (proceeds; a compensating task is filed for the objecting artifact).
7. **Termination.** Bounded: one position round + one rebuttal round, then the adjudicator must decide or escalate along the parent edge. Unbounded deliberation is a defect.
8. **Record.** One adjudication event in the feed: the request, the affected set, every position, fidelity challenges + outcomes, the decision + rationale, the authority record. Every position and decision is a graded prediction (R2).
9. **Escalation.** Any party may escalate a decision one level along the parent edge (at root: the human). An escalation is itself an ordinary change request, re-heard under this same procedure by the parent's embodiment.

### Actions x who is convened

| Action | Route | Convened | Decided by |
|---|---|---|---|
| PR merge (uncontested) | sign-off judges -> approver adopts | nobody — single approver embodiment | plan's `approver` config |
| PR merge (contested) | procedure above | mover + affected artifacts + adjudicator | adjudicator (owning node's embodiment) |
| Promotion (merge/pin) | promotion PR at the parent | nobody while uncontested | that edge's approver |
| `pm sub create` / `fork` / `watch` / `plan register` | law 3, approval-free | nobody | n/a |
| `pm sub raise` (scope expansion) | procedure above, always | containing parent's plan + mover-named affected pieces | adjudicated per procedure |
| Governance change | ordinary request | nobody unless contested | holding project's current approver config (root: human) |
| Certification request | ordinary request to registry-holding project | nobody — evidence bundle speaks | that project's adoption (root: human) |
| Outbound crossing | crossing-request queue | nobody unless contested | human, or a granted process |
| Fidelity challenge | inline in any proceeding | n/a — read the artifact |

Mechanics per edge: a **containing** edge is a git branch carrying its own committed `pm/`; a delegated result-record promotion is the root's plan watcher executing `git merge stream/ibex` + `git checkout master -- pm/` (the metadata-restore rule) once the edge's `process@eval-verify` condition is met by the record PR's re-execution evidence, writing authority record + feed event. **A4. The first lapse and re-certification — run cheap, on the cycles lineage. Unmanned except the re-adoption.**

The human re-adopts the grant; the root watcher re-points the configs.

**B. A raise — rung M5. Attended.** The sweeps stream finds its constraint-tuning generalizes beyond this campaign -> `pm sub raise orfs-tuning-lab` (creates the new project, registers the promoting edge). The convocation spawns embodiments of the affected pieces (campaign root plan, eval-pipeline plan), prompts compiled from plan text + node summary + log. **C. Sharing across humans — rung M5's edge operations; cross-user transport is plain git + text.** Another person's project takes a **watch** edge or **forks** a subproject. Their improvements arrive as ordinary requests — per one-request-shape, literally a text file or PR into the child's queue. No shared infrastructure, no accounts, no position granted to anyone.

A matured patch's upstream PR is the shadow's outbound crossing: a crossing-request record surfaces in the feed, and on the human's agreement the PR is opened via the existing `gh` path from a credentialed session outside the containers.

## Protocol-designn recommendations — theorized, yet to be proven

Each held as a claim, `theorized` until exercise grades it; proof conditions named so graduation is mechanical.

- **R1 — No unexaminable claims about reality.** Outcomes as performed checks, never self-reports (the campaign's `process@eval-verify`: independent re-execution or no front eligibility); environment through auditable structure (plan-radar's per-metric triage); self-model claims (`verified`/`believed`/`contested`) carrying falsification handles, with evaluations commissionable against them. **Proven when**: seeded-dishonest exams show sustained discrimination and no silent-corruption incident over a full campaign quarter.
- **R2 — Everything is a graded prediction.** Verdicts, adoptions, adjudications, certifications, fidelity judgments, summary claims — logged with outcomes; miss-loops file fixtures. **Proven when**: exam-driven prompt/model changes measurably reduce false-PASS/false-block on the #160-lineage fixtures.
- **R3 — Sustain/override/absorb beats voting and veto.** Preserves objection information without majority averaging or incumbent ossification. **Proven when**: override-grading shows calibrated adjudicators and absorbed tasks show objections were productive rather than steamrolled.
- **R4 — Rank reading surfaces by grounded credibility, never engagement.** Plan-momentum's law applied to the project feed; per-reader minimal-sufficient projections. **Proven when**: surfaced-item acted-on/led-to-progress rates hold as volume grows.
- **R5 — Governance cost scales with disagreement.** Routine adoptions are a single embodiment; convocations only when contested. **Proven when**: agreement overhead stays sublinear in merges at swarm scale.
- **R6 — Plain text is the bedrock; form never gates and never entitles.** The one-request-shape and communication paragraphs held as a bet: keeping requests and responses generic — any form admissible, none required, none privileged — is the durable defense against process exploitation and form-borne standing, at an accepted cost in machine-parseability. **Proven when**: no request or response is refused or discounted for its form alone, no form-based exploitation incident occurs, and structured conventions keep earning voluntary use.
- **R7 — The human's attention is the binding constraint on feed quality.** Compress, don't just rank: produce the minimal event projection that preserves decision-relevant information at human reading speed. **Proven when**: human decisions per digest item stay constant as event volume scales 10x.
- **R8 — A node summary alone must support minimal-sufficient onboarding.** A fresh embodiment reading only the stream's summary + log tail must reach productive orientation within one decision. **Proven when**: mean time-to-first-productive-git-commit for new embodiments stays flat as tree depth grows.
- **R9 — Representation fidelity is graded separately from outcome correctness.** Fidelity error rates are published per model/prompt/artifact-class — the calibration ledger's raw material. **Proven when**: fidelity-degraded embodiments are detected and respawned before causing an adoption error.

## MVP

**pm can run the campaign unattended for a week**: watchers pull problems from the queue, agents complete work in containers, sub-streams adopt each other's work recursively, changes merge only through one of Protocol v0's authorities — a human, the crossed node's embodiment, or a certified process within its grant — every merge records its authority, and the human reads a trustworthy feed. The human touches exactly: outbound crossings, whatever adoptions are configured `human` (the root project and anything not yet delegated, root governance changes included), and resolution items from scope expansions.

**Sequencing**: build Set A here -> bootstrap the sister project -> work both in parallel (Set B here; campaign tiers there).

### Sub-MVPs

- **M0 — commit the corpus** (prerequisite zero): this plan registered in `project.yaml`; the sibling-plan edits committed; riscv-pareto's first commit. The constitution of adoption gets its first adoption.
- **M1 — authority on the existing path**: land/stabilize pr-ff9b728 + pr-ed10ac4; the feed ledger and the authority envelope wrap the *existing* merge path; the process registry lands with exact scopes, lapse, and manual revoke. Run **attended, on pm's own bugs/improvements plans** — the dogfood exit criterion: authority-recorded merges and a nightly digest before any campaign work. Result: the first trustworthy feed, within days (worked example A0).
- **M2 — one child, one promotion**: a single branch-rooted subproject with one containing edge and one merge-promotion (metadata restore included); node work logs + claim-tagged short summaries (the verification-problem loop stays Set B). Result: the maintainer-tree model demonstrated end to end at n=1 (worked example A1).
- **M3 — cycles-only front** (campaign sub-MVP): one core, Verilator only, perf axis only -> stamped records, a provisional front, every merge human-adopted; then the **first certification rehearsal**: eval-verify certified on the cycles pipeline, where independent re-execution costs minutes. Result: a live front on real measurements before ORFS exists, and the certification machinery exercised cheaply (worked example A2; the first lapse/re-cert drill, A4, runs here too).
- **M4 — full-physical lineage**: the ORFS `backend_version` lands as a new lineage with its re-eval wave; the energy axis joins the front; the two-config grant flip runs for real.
- **M5 — growth machinery**: `on_missing: create_sub`, plural edges (raise / fork / watch), parent-agent adoption in subtrees, all-core fan-out — and the full convocation procedure armed behind its build trigger, the first contested case (worked example A3 is that trigger made concrete).

Each slice's completion is judged by exercised evidence in the feed, never by merged PRs alone.

**Built on plan-regression Phase 11, not beside it.** Sign-off (pr-2d5f712, merged as #225) is the judge: it already reviews all cross-stage evidence, records `{verdict, sha, ts, origin}`, and routes per-PR — recommending, never merging. The plan auto-start watcher (pr-ff9b728, pending) is the adoption actor: it acts on sign-off's recommendations per plan config, caps in-flight work, and mutates the plan as reality diverges. Set A layers identity, grants, audit, and tree-escalation on those pieces and generalizes three of Phase 11's assumptions:

1. *The flat repo becomes recursive.* A subproject is a full pm project rooted in a separate repo or in a branch of the containing repo that acts for it exactly as master does — the Linux-kernel maintainer-tree model, one `base_branch` indirection.
2. *The binary flag becomes an approver config.* Phase 11's per-plan `gated|autonomous` flag generalizes to `approver: human | parent-agent | process@grant`.
3. *Plan notes become node self-models.* Phase 11's watcher continuity (plan notes) extends into a per-node work log + maintained summary made of claims with falsification handles, staleness-checked at promotion.

**Explicit non-dependencies**, accepted as risk to keep the pre-loop set minimal: plan-regression Phase 10 (regressions-as-scenarios) and the bridge (pr-fbda1a8) — the campaign's QA is eval-pipeline-shaped, so the loop gets validated by the campaign itself; and the mind+sensorium refactor — the feed ledger is a proto-EmissionLog, kept off the refactor's critical path.

## PRs — Set A: required before the campaign launches

(Each PR names the pm machinery it extends, the tests it requires, and its dependencies. Full technical detail is in the original plan; this section is a summary.)

### PR: Certified-process registry + merge authority records

`pm/processes.yaml` registry: `{id, kind, version: hash(prompts+model config+flow), grant: [change-classes/plans], status: certified|lapsed, evidence: [run refs]}`. Layers on Phase 11: the plan auto-start watcher's per-plan config generalizes from `gated|autonomous` to `approver: human | parent-agent | process@<id>`, and the authority record extends sign-off's existing `{verdict, sha, ts, origin}` record with `authority: human | agent:<stream> | process@version`, written into project state and the feed ledger on every merge. Modifying a registered process definition auto-lapses its grant pending re-certification — filed and adopted as an ordinary change request to the holding project. CLI: `pm process list/show/certify/lapse`. Requires pr-ff9b728 landed or co-developed.

### PR: Project feed v1 — append-only event ledger + digest

`pm/feed/events.jsonl` append-only ledger. Event types: merge (with authority), verdict, escalation/adjudication, crossing-request, external (generic payload — campaign front-deltas arrive this way), digest. Writers at merge/sign-off/adjudication sites. `pm feed` CLI (tail/filter); daily digest generator. Events are proto-Emissions: field shape kept compatible with plan-mind's `Emission` envelope so the ledger folds into `EmissionLog` when the refactor lands — the refactor is deliberately not a dependency.

### PR: Plan-tree approval + escalation — the parent embodiment as approver

Extends pr-ff9b728. (1) When `approver` is `parent-agent`, an approver session **embodying the parent plan** (spawned from its canonical state per v0's Embodiment) decides each `ready_to_merge`: it reads the sign-off report + evidence + node summary and adopts or declines with reasons — the instance persists either way. The same duty decides promotions at subproject boundaries. Sign-off remains the judge; the approver is the adoption. (2) Escalation along parent edges; root escalations reach the human via feed + crossing queue. (3) Explicit adjudication events: every adopt/sustain/override/absorb lands in the feed so approver judgment is gradeable against grounded outcome and representation fidelity is gradeable separately.

### PR: Pluggable problem sources for discovery watchers

Generalize the discovery supervisor (#174 pattern): a command or file contract emitting problem records `{id, title, rationale, tier, refs, target: {project|subproject, plan}, scope: pr|subproject, on_missing: create_sub|hold}` — a problem names the node it lands in; `on_missing: create_sub` routes through `pm sub create` first. The campaign's front-gap analyzer is the first external source; pm's own bugs/improvements discovery becomes the reference implementation of the contract.

### PR: Agent-initiated sub-plan creation (non-interactive)

`pm plan register <file> --parent <plan-id>` — registers an existing plan markdown as a child plan directly (no Claudee session), using the merged `parent` primitivess (#150/#151). Permitted from free-tier sessions so streams can open sub-streams on their own judgment; registration emits a feed event.

### PR: Crossing-request records (outbound agreement queue)

First-class record for action crossings: `{id, kind: push_external|publish|spend|other, description, evidence refs, state: requested|granted|denied, authority}`. At MVP all outbound crossings require the human's agreement; containers already hold no external creds. Forward-compatible with plan-mind's `AttentionRequest`/`consult(human)`.

### PR: Subprojects — branch-rooted or separate-repo + promotion PRs

A **subproject** is a full pm project whose root is either (a) a separate repo, or (b) a branch of the containing repo that acts for it exactly as master does today. `base_branch` per project; `pm sub create <name> --branch | --repo <path>`; promotion into the parent is a PR in the parent project whose head is the child's base branch — the promotion merge **restores the parent's `pm/`** (child metadata never lands on the parent base). Four **approval-free** operations: `pm sub create`, `pm sub raise`, `pm sub fork`, `pm sub watch`. Plural membership (law 3): parent links are per-edge records; a subproject can belong to several parents at once. Mechanics detailed in the original plan.

### PR: Node work logs + maintained summaries

Every node keeps an append-only **work log** (every session touching the node appends a one-line entry) and a **maintained summary** of addressable **claims**, each tagged `verified` / `believed` / `contested`, plus direction and open questions. The summary is maximally load-bearing — every embodiment is spawned from this state (v0's Embodiment), so a wrong summary corrupts every representative the node sends. Verification requests file through the problem-source contract; a falsification is a feed event. Both artifacts are markdown in the project's `pm/`, readable by humans and agents alike. Depends on Subprojects + Pluggable problem sources.

### PR: Trust-prompt clearing — context-aware recovery playbook

Unattended operation dies on workspace-trust prompts (Claudee Code's do-you-trust-this-folder confirmation). The session-health watcher (pr-18ac983/#184) detects a session stalled on a trust prompt, verifies the workspace path is one pm provisioned, and accepts it — never a global `--dangerously-skip-permissions` bypass. Relocated from the watchers plan per its review note.
